# Official sources

Use these for every year-specific value. Do not rely on memory.

- Law texts: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/ (EStG), https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/ (AO), https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/ (StBerG)
- Income tax guidelines (EStR/EStH): https://esth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/
- Wage tax guidelines (LStR/LStH): https://lsth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/
- Federal Ministry of Finance: https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/
- ELSTER forms and help: https://www.elster.de/

The exact pages behind each threshold are listed in `thresholds.json`.
